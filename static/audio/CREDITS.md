## Checking a new sound's license

1. Use the license on the creator's own page, not a re-upload's description.
2. CC0 is the easiest: no conditions. CC BY is fine too, but its attribution must be shown in the app,
   not only in this file. Don't use CC BY-NC (no commercial use), CC BY-ND (no changes) or
   anything marked "personal use only".
3. Record the creator, source link, license, and any changes you made in the tables above.
